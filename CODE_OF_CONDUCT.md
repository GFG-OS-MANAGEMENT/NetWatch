# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

NetWatch welcomes contributors of all experience levels. We are committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in the project.

## Expected Behaviour

All contributors are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and professionalism.
- Welcome constructive feedback and different perspectives.
- Focus discussions on ideas, code quality, and project goals.
- Give credit for other people's work.
- Help beginners learn and participate.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and work toward resolving disagreements constructively.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, humiliating, or deliberately intimidating contributors.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Disrupting discussions or reviews through repeated abusive behaviour.
- Using the project to promote unauthorized monitoring or harmful cybersecurity activity.

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the project maintainers through an appropriate private communication channel. Reports should be handled respectfully and reviewed fairly.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests, documentation, and other spaces where contributors represent or participate in NetWatch.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for addressing reported concerns and may take appropriate action, including warnings, requests to change behaviour, or restriction of participation.

Our goal is to maintain a safe, constructive environment where students can collaborate and learn.

Thank you for helping make NetWatch a welcoming open-source learning project.
